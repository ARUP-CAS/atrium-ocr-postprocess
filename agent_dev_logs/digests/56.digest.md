## ✅ What's settled
- **The new name** is `ufal/atrium-ocr-postprocess` (report §2.2; this issue).
- **The pipeline is kept**: steps, scripts, categories and scoring do not change with the name.
- **Its stage in AMČR's chain** does not change: stored and new OCR output (PSNC ALTO, HPC data, the ATR service)
  goes to `/process`; born-digital lines come back to it for scoring (llm-enrich#10 W3).
